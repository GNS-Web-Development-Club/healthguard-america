The five modules

Three are open to the public; RuralAccess and ChronicCare are limited to the government portal.

PriceVision

Compare hospital procedure prices across 5,400+ facilities. See cash prices, insurance rates, and price variations.

  • 30M price records
  • All 50 states covered
  • Cash and negotiated rates side by side
Explore prices

DrugWatch

Track drug prices across the US, Australia, and Canada. See Medicare spending and international price comparisons.

  • 500+ high-spending drugs
  • International comparisons
  • $276B Medicare spending data
Compare drugs

FoodScore

Check health scores for 50,000+ food products. NOVA classification, additive risks, and MAHA scores.

  • 50,000+ products analyzed
  • ML-assisted NOVA classification
  • Additive risk scoring
Check foods

RuralAccess

Map healthcare shortage areas (HPSAs) across America. Identify underserved communities and access gaps.

  • 1M+ healthcare providers
  • 14,631 shortage designations
  • 2,956 counties analyzed
View map

ChronicCare

Analyze chronic disease patterns by county. ML-assisted intervention prioritization and MAHA index.

  • 1.2M CDC records
  • 3,142 counties analyzed
  • Intervention recommendations
Analyze data

Raw data
